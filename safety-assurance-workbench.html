Solutions / Technology product

Safety Assurance Workbench

A human-led environment for converting reported autonomous-vehicle incidents into evidence-linked investigation records and reviewable safety documentation.

Development status

Working prototype · Human acceptance testing pending

Automated integration testing has passed (166 tests in the latest recorded run). Independent three-person user acceptance testing is prepared but has not yet been completed. This is not a production-certified safety system.

Incident-to-report workflow

01 · Incident IntakeCapture an original reporter account and structured follow-up answers.
02 · Initial Incident ReportReview submissions, establish case linkage, and retain source context.
03 · Detailed InvestigationRegister evidence, document hypotheses and limitations, record human review and safety decisions.
04 · Final ReportExport a dossier with traceable source references, decisions, and unresolved questions.

What the prototype demonstrates

Evidence traceability

Source-linked records, provenance metadata, and review histories help a reviewer follow the reasoning.

Human judgment and uncertainty

Unverified observations remain distinct from established facts. Missing evidence and alternative explanations are documented.

Safety decision support

Analysts can record provisional or unresolved assessments and identify evidence needed before stronger conclusions.

Auditable reporting

Generated HTML case reports connect substantive findings to the available records and limitations.

What it does not claim

The Workbench does not automatically detect incidents, independently authenticate uploaded evidence, establish vehicle causation, certify autonomous-system safety, or make binding regulatory determinations. Operational deployment, access controls, security, and independent user acceptance require additional validation.
