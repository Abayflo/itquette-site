Independent Research · Autonomous Vehicle Safety & Operations

We study how autonomous
vehicle operations fail,
recover, and improve

ITquette Autonomous Lab is an independent research platform focused on incident management, safety lifecycle governance, and operational resilience in autonomous vehicle deployments. Our work is designed to help operators, builders, and regulators think more clearly about the problems that scale creates.

The technology is no longer the primary bottleneck. The incident frameworks, evidence readiness, safety governance, and operational protocols are. As deployments scale from thousands to hundreds of thousands of rides, the gap between what operators have built and what they actually need is widening. That is what our research examines.

Three areas our research focuses on

01

Incident Lifecycle & Response

How AV operators structure incident response from detection through closure — what works, what breaks under scale, and what distinguishes organisations that improve consistently from those that repeat failure modes.

Triage protocols Escalation logic After action reviews Corrective actions
02

Evidence Readiness & Regulator Narratives

How operators prepare for regulatory scrutiny — what evidence gets captured, how it gets packaged, and whether the narrative that reaches a regulator accurately represents what happened and what changed.

Evidence packaging Redaction protocols Regulator narratives Privacy frameworks
03

Safety Governance & Traceability

How organisations maintain safety claims over time as systems evolve, markets expand, and fleets scale — with a focus on the traceability gap between field performance, corrective actions, and updated safety cases.

UL 4600 alignment Safety case maintenance Change traceability Multi-market consistency

Latest insights

Short, practical notes on safety lifecycle management and operational resilience. No hype. Just what holds up in real deployments.

Research note

Incidents are sequences, not moments

Most breakdowns happen after the event — in triage, handoffs, evidence packaging, and follow-up. Trust improves when this lifecycle is consistent and auditable.
Research note

UL 4600 is a safety argument that must be maintained

Compliance friction appears in traceability between incidents and safety case updates, and alignment between operational data and safety claims.
Research note

Trust is cumulative for cities and regulators

Stakeholders ask what changed afterward, how recurrence is prevented, and how consistency is maintained across markets. Evidence readiness makes those conversations calmer.
