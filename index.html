The challenge

Why incident management breaks at scale

As deployments grow and scrutiny increases, ad-hoc processes crumble. You need repeatable systems that connect incidents to evidence to improvement.

⚠️

Coordination debt

Handoffs between ops, engineering, policy, and comms become slow and inconsistent.

  • ❌ Unclear ownership and escalation paths
  • ❌ Inconsistent severity categorization
  • ❌ Delayed decisions from missing context
High impact Adds 3-7 days per incident
📁

Evidence readiness

Reconstruction is manual, time-consuming, and impossible to audit.

  • ❌ Artifacts scattered across tools and teams
  • ❌ Manual redaction and privacy compliance
  • ❌ No regulator-ready packaging
Critical risk Regulatory exposure
📈

Learning that doesn't scale

Without traceability, lessons learned don't translate to safer operations over time.

  • ❌ Corrective actions lose visibility
  • ❌ Changes aren't linked to outcomes
  • ❌ Cross-market consistency unproven

Our approach

Lifecycle thinking, operational focus

We treat incident response as a lifecycle with clear phases, ownership, and outputs. The goal: clarity during crises, measurable improvement afterward.

1

Intake & Triage

Define severity, impact, and escalation paths. Assign owners immediately.

2

Evidence & Reconstruction

Preserve context, collect artifacts, maintain chain of custody.

3

After-Action Review

Root cause analysis, contributing factors, systemic patterns.

4

Corrective Actions

Track, own, close. Link changes to outcomes.

5

Governance & Reporting

Maintain traceability to safety claims. Scale across markets.
