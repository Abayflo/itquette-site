Safety Investigation Pilot

A proposed, carefully scoped engagement to evaluate how ITquette's investigation and evidence-assurance approach fits an autonomous mobility organization's operational needs.

Engagement status

In preparation · Not yet launched

We're preparing a collaborative evaluation opportunity for selected organizations interested in evidence-led safety investigations. Contact ITquette to discuss potential participation and evaluation goals.

Proposed engagement sequence

01 · DiscoveryUnderstand the organization's incident documentation and evidence needs.
02 · ScopeAgree on test scenarios, participants, boundaries, and success criteria.
03 · Controlled evaluationWalk through selected fictional or otherwise authorized scenarios with human reviewers.
04 · FindingsDocument usability, traceability, gaps, and recommended next steps.

Safeguards before operational data

Any future use of customer or operational records would require appropriate authorization, confidentiality and data handling agreements, privacy and security review, source provenance controls, independent review, and a documented go/no-go decision. No live-data access or production integration is implied by an initial conversation.

Who may benefit

Autonomous mobility operators, fleet safety teams, incident investigators, and organizations evaluating evidence-driven safety governance.
